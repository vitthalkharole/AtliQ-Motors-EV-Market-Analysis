# 🚗 AtliQ Motors – EV Market Analysis



---

## 📌 Project Overview

AtliQ Motors is an automotive company planning to expand its Electric Vehicle (EV) business in India.

This project analyzes the Indian EV market from **FY2022 to FY2024** to understand:

- EV market growth
- 2-Wheeler and 4-Wheeler sales
- Manufacturer performance
- State-wise EV penetration
- State-level growth
- Market competition
- Future EV market opportunities

The project follows an end-to-end **Data Analyst workflow**, starting from data cleaning and SQL analysis and ending with an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Identify the top 3 and bottom 3 2-Wheeler EV manufacturers.
2. Identify states with the highest EV penetration.
3. Find states where EV penetration declined.
4. Analyze quarterly EV sales trends by manufacturer.
5. Compare EV performance between Delhi and Karnataka.
6. Calculate manufacturer CAGR.
7. Calculate state-level EV sales CAGR.
8. Identify peak and low EV sales months.
9. Project potential EV sales for 2030 using historical CAGR.
10. Analyze EV sales and revenue growth.

---

# 🏢 Business Problem

AtliQ Motors has a strong presence in the international EV market but wants to understand the Indian market before expanding further.

The company needs data-driven answers to questions such as:

- Which EV manufacturers are leading the market?
- Which states have higher EV adoption?
- Which states are growing faster?
- How is the 2W market different from the 4W market?
- Where are the potential opportunities for future expansion?
- How has the EV market changed between FY2022 and FY2024?

This project uses data analysis and visualization to answer these questions.

## 💼 Business Insights

- **2W EVs dominate** the market, making them a key segment for analysis.
- **EV adoption varies by state**, so market strategy should be state-specific.
- **Competition is changing**, especially among 2W manufacturers.
- **High CAGR does not always mean a large market**; it should be viewed with sales volume and penetration.
- **2W and 4W require different strategies** due to different market patterns.
- **High-growth states** can be potential future markets, subject to infrastructure and policy validation.
